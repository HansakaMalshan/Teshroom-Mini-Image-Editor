## ✨ Features Included

*   **File Operations:** Easily **Open** and **Save** images in popular formats (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.bmp`, `.gif`).
*   **One-Click Undo:** Made a mistake? Reset your changes instantly with the **Undo** button.
*   **Rotation & Flipping:** 
    *   Rotate 90 degrees Left or Right.
    *   Flip horizontally (H) or vertically (V).
*   **Smart Filters:**
    *   **Grayscale:** Convert any colorful image into elegant black and white.
    *   **Blur:** Apply a soft focus blur filter to your images.
*   **Precise Adjustments (Sliders):**
    *   Interactive slider to fine-tune **Brightness**.
    *   Interactive slider to control **Contrast** levels.
*   **Responsive UI:** Beautiful dark mode interface with modern custom buttons and an auto-scaling canvas that perfectly fits your screen.
